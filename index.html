About CampusConnect

Institutional project archiving, academic transparency, and verified team collaboration.

Built for Academic Discovery

Final-year capstone projects represent months of engineering design, prototyping, and scholarly inquiry. CampusConnect preserves these projects in a centralized repository so students, faculty advisors, and visiting industry reviewers can explore prior work across all six departments.

Every project, student & faculty achievement, placement record, and team contact channel published on CampusConnect undergoes faculty and administrator review for United Institute of Technology before appearing in the public catalog.
